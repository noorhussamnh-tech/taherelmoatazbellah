About

An engineer by training, a political scientist by instinct, and a storyteller by trade. He turns history and politics into stories people can’t stop watching.

Taher El Moataz Bellah is the screenwriter and bestselling author behind some of the most-watched and most-listened Arabic stories on YouTube, Storytel, Shahid and in print.

He wrote 30 episodes of El Da7ee7, the biggest online show in the Arab world, including its most-watched episode. His books profile the people who bent history: a Russian president, a British princess, two rival icons of Black America, and the geniuses who changed how the world writes and thinks.

Before writing full time he was a drilling engineer at BP. He holds an MBA from Harvard Business School and now runs Heartbeat Productions, a film studio taking Arab stories to global audiences.
